About HireWise

HireWise is a final-year project: a knowledge graph-enhanced hybrid recommender for digital worker-service platforms. Recommendations combine profile matching, collaborative hiring patterns and knowledge-graph evidence, and were evaluated on a research dataset derived from real Upwork job postings.
